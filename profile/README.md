## Clinical software that gets out of your way

Aster Clinical is being built around the small frictions that accumulate during real consultations: repeated entry, scattered records, awkward printing, and software that asks the doctor to adapt to it.

Our aim is simpler: focused tools that keep the clinical visit together, reduce clerical friction, and stay out of the way of clinical work.

## Current work

| Project | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Aster Clinical** | A desktop clinical application for patient records, structured visits, prescription workflow, investigations, medication entry, visit history, and print-ready clinical output. The first product is currently in invite-only beta. |
| **[Clinical Document Platform Format (CDPF)](https://github.com/AsterClinical/clinical-document-platform-format)** | A public specification for structured clinical documents and interoperable clinical-document workflows. |
| **[Aster Clinical Brand Assets](https://github.com/AsterClinical/brand-assets)** | The canonical home of Aster's mascot family, compact cat marks, typography development assets, provenance records, and public brand-use guidance. |

## How we work

**Practical by default.** Features should solve real workflow problems before they add complexity.

**Clinician-led.** Aster starts from the realities of everyday clinical practice rather than an abstract enterprise workflow.

**Open where it helps.** Standards, formats, and selected components can be developed in public when openness improves interoperability, scrutiny, or reuse.

**Brand identity stays distinct.** Open-source or documentation licences do not automatically grant rights to use the Aster Clinical name, cat marks, mascot, or other brand identifiers. See the [brand guidelines](https://github.com/AsterClinical/brand-assets/blob/main/BRAND_GUIDELINES.md) and [trademark policy](https://github.com/AsterClinical/brand-assets/blob/main/TRADEMARK_POLICY.md).
